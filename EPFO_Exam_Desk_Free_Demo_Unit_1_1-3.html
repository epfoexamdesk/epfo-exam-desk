Welcome to Unit 1.1. This module covers the foundational statutory and administrative framework of India's formal sector social security architecture.

Learning Objectives:

  • Understand the establishment and statutory foundation of the EPFO under the EPF & MP Act, 1952 and the Code on Social Security, 2020 [2, 3].
  • Analyze the administrative structure, including the tripartite Central Board of Trustees (CBT) and its executive functions [2, 4].
  • Compare the core features, eligibility, and contributions of the EPF, EPS, and EDLI schemes [5-7].
  • Evaluate recent administrative reforms, digital initiatives (like UAN and CPPS), and the ELI Scheme (PM-VBRY) [3, 7, 8].
  • Map legacy statutory provisions to their corresponding chapters and sections in the new Code on Social Security, 2020 [3, 9, 10].

EPFO Organisational Structure & Concepts

The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) is a statutory body under the administrative control of the Ministry of Labour and Employment [2].

Central Board of Trustees (CBT)

Under Section 5A of the 1952 Act (and Section 4(1) of the 2020 Code), the CBT is a tripartite body corporate having perpetual succession and a common seal [2].

Clickable Concept: CBT Composition (Max 43 Members)

  • Chairman & Vice-Chairman: Appointed by the Central Government [2].
  • CPFC: Ex-officio member and Chief Executive Officer [2].
  • Central Govt Reps: Not more than 5 persons [2].
  • State Govt Reps: Not more than 15 persons [2].
  • Employers' Reps: 10 persons [2].
  • Employees' Reps: 10 persons [2].

Applicability Thresholds

The Act applies to establishments employing 20 or more persons [11]. However, there is a specific exemption for co-operative societies. Under Section 16(1)(a), the Act excludes establishments registered under the Co-operative Societies Act, 1912 employing less than 50 persons AND working without the aid of power [11, 12]. Both conditions are cumulative [13].

Comparison of EPF, EPS, and EDLI Schemes

Together, these three form an integrated three-tier social security shield from a single employer remittance [14].

Revision & Common Examination Traps

Key Facts:

  • Origin: EPF Ordinance (15 Nov 1951) → EPF & MP Act, 1952 (4 Mar 1952) → Code on Social Security, 2020 (effective 21 Nov 2025, as stated in supplied notes).
  • Auto-settlement of advances: Launched April 2020 at ₹50,000 → enhanced to ₹1 lakh (April 2024) → enhanced to ₹5 lakh (June 2025).
  • ELI Scheme / PM-VBRY: ~₹1 lakh crore outlay. Employers must add ≥ 2 employees (if <50 staff) or ≥ 5 employees (if ≥50 staff) sustained for ≥ 6 months to qualify for Part B.

Common Examination Traps:

Trap 1: Threshold Confusion
Do not mix up 20 (Sec. 1(3) normal establishment), 50 (Sec. 16(1)(a) cooperative without power), and 100 (Sec. 16A separate PF account).

Trap 2: EPS Contributions
Employees contribute only to EPF (12%)—never to EPS or EDLI (0%).

Trap 3: Appeal Pre-Deposit
Under legacy Section 7-O it was 75%; under Section 23(2) of the Code on Social Security, 2020, it is 25%.
