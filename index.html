EPFO Organisational Structure & Concepts

The Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) administers provident-fund, pension and deposit-linked insurance arrangements under the Ministry of Labour and Employment. The Code on Social Security, 2020 provides the current statutory framework.

Central Board of Trustees (CBT)

Code section 4(1) — composition structure
  • Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson.
  • Up to five Central Government representatives.
  • Up to fifteen State Government representatives.
  • Ten employer representatives.
  • Ten employee representatives.
  • Central Provident Fund Commissioner as ex-officio member/Chief Executive Officer under the relevant Code provision.

The arithmetic maximum is 43 when both Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson are counted. Use the precise statutory wording when answering a composition question.

Coverage and co-operative society exclusion

Chapter III coverage ordinarily applies at the 20-employee threshold. The co-operative-society exclusion in section 20(1)(a) requires both conditions: fewer than 50 persons and working without the aid of power. The two conditions are cumulative.

EPS calculation memory aid

For a member other than an existing member: monthly pension = pensionable wages × pensionable service ÷ 70. Pensionable wages are based on the relevant 60-month period and are subject to the wage ceiling applicable to each wage-ceiling period. Read the exceptions and transitional provisions before applying the formula.

EDLI benefit — do not memorise only the headline range

Para 21 includes an average-PF-balance-linked benefit and a wage-linked formula: average monthly wages (subject to ceiling) × 35 plus 50% of average PF balance subject to ₹1.75 lakh; the benefit under that formula is subject to ₹2.5 lakh minimum and ₹7 lakh maximum, with the additional 20% provision in para 21(5). Apply the scheme's “whichever is higher” and eligibility wording.

Revision & Common Examination Traps

Trap 1 — Three different wage references

EPF notified wage ceiling: ₹25,000. EPS Central Government contribution ceiling: ₹15,000. Do not use one figure for both.

Trap 2 — Contributions

EPF: employer 12% and employee equal contribution. EPS: employer 8.33% to Pension Fund plus Central Government 1.16% on wages up to ₹15,000. EDLI: employer 0.5%; no employee EDLI contribution.

Trap 3 — Appeal deposit

Code section 23(3) provides the 25% pre-deposit. Section 23(1) identifies the Tribunal. Do not cite section 23(2).

Trap 4 — Thresholds

20 employees: ordinary Chapter III coverage threshold. Co-operative society exclusion: fewer than 50 persons AND no aid of power. Separate PF account: section 21, 100 or more persons subject to statutory conditions.

Trap 5 — Current vs legacy

1952 EPF Scheme, 1995 EPS and 1976 EDLI are historical/legacy instruments; the 2026 schemes are the current notified scheme texts. Read corrigenda/amendments together.
